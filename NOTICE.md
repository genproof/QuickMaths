QuickMaths is released under the MIT License ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). The third-party components below keep their own
licences.

The harness fetches third-party sources at pinned commits at run time and does not vendor them. Run logs,
datasets, account material and per-problem operator notes stay outside the repository by design
(docs/SECURITY.md).

- `runtime/leaneval/resources/benchmark.json` is derived from the public LeanEval benchmark
  (https://github.com/leanprover/lean-eval, Apache-2.0): the problem catalog (ids, titles, hole statements,
  sources) of the v1 set at commit 208b6b22d396f28771dba56414b19f47cae5f2a7, generated by
  `tools/make_catalog.py` from a checkout of that commit.
- `runtime/tools/build_workbench.py` clones the benchmark at the pin in
  `runtime/leaneval/resources/verifier-pins.json` and installs landrun
  (https://github.com/Zouuup/landrun) at its pinned revision with `go install`.
- `runtime/tools/build_bwrap.sh` fetches the organizers' `scripts/install_bwrap.sh` of
  https://github.com/leanprover/lean-eval-submissions at commit 4704027e5e221bbe8b0f8356dec1ce5c1965fd6e
  (Apache-2.0, SHA-256 c290df6cebdf1cb26edb69ed4164d9551062bfeb65243b93d1d1d3b77eac0a35), which downloads and
  builds the bubblewrap 0.12.0 release (https://github.com/containers/bubblewrap, LGPL-2.0-or-later) after
  checking its SHA-256. When libcap's headers are missing it unpacks the distribution's libcap-dev package next
  to the build.
- `verify/` fetches the organizers' evaluator `scripts/evaluate_submission.py` of lean-eval-submissions at
  commit 0b38814ed5606268e450df923f814e1613cf1ae7 (Apache-2.0) and checks its SHA-256
  (4619d7475ceb17ec6be68a2490db6d1465a8fdff30668c7554504fda9755f7b2).
- `runtime/tools/bank.py` clones the solution bank's sources at the commits the operator names and accepts
  only the licences of the profile's source policy (Apache-2.0 and MIT by default).
